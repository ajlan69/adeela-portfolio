About Me

Understanding every learner, one at a time

I'm a counseling professional with more than six years of experience in remedial training, academic counseling, and student support. I run an independent home-based learning setup in Kannur that has impacted 50+ students — each supported through carefully built Individualized Learning Plans (ILPs) tailored to how they learn best.

My work blends structured assessment with empathetic, research-informed counseling. I believe academic growth and emotional well-being go hand in hand, so I partner closely with parents and educators to reinforce consistent, caring support at home and in school.

Currently pursuing a Master of Arts in Psychology (IGNOU) alongside an Advanced Diploma in Counseling Psychology, I'm deepening my clinical training to serve children, adolescents, and families with even greater care.
